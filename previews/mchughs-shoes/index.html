Tap the shoe

Where's the wear?

Every pair wears in its own places. Tap where yours is going, see what usually fixes it, and start a quote with the repair already picked.

The heel

Worn down at the back.

  • Heel blocks or top pieces worn to an angle
  • Rubber worn through to the nail — you can hear it click
  • Heels that have come loose

Look at the heel from behind: if it slopes, it’s time.

Quote this repair

The sole

Thin, split or slippery.

  • Holes or soft spots at the ball of the foot
  • Soles lifting away from the upper
  • Smooth soles that need grip — sole protectors

Leather soles are best done before a hole goes right through.

Quote this repair

Seams & stitching

Gaps that keep growing.

  • Split seams along the side or toe
  • Welt stitching coming undone
  • Straps and buckles pulling away

A gap fixed early is a smaller job than a gap left for a season.

Quote this repair

Collar & lining

Rubbing you the wrong way.

  • Lining worn through at the heel
  • Heels slipping — heel grips can help
  • Torn collar padding

Photograph the inside of the heel — that’s where the wear hides.

Quote this repair

Toe & upper

Scuffed, faded, tired.

  • Scuffed toes and faded colour
  • Dry, cracked leather that needs feeding
  • Salt or water marks

Shoot it in daylight so the true colour shows.
