The repair guide

Common repairs, explained.

Every pair is different, so prices are quoted per job — never guessed from a list. Find your problem, then tap “Quote this” to start a request with it ticked.

Heels & heel tips

Fixes
Worn-down heels, clicking metal, lost or split tips on stilettos.
Good to know
You can hear the nail, or the heel is wearing at an angle.
Quote this

Half soles & resoles

Fixes
Thin, holed or slippery soles on leather and rubber-soled shoes.
Good to know
The sole feels soft at the ball of the foot, or water gets in.
Quote this

Stitching & gluing

Fixes
Split seams, a sole coming away, loose straps and lifting edges.
Good to know
You see a gap opening — it’s easier to fix early.
Quote this

Boot zips

Fixes
Broken, stuck or splitting zips on boots and booties.
Good to know
The zip separates behind the slider, or teeth are missing.
Quote this

Stretching

Fixes
Shoes that pinch across the width, or tight boot calves.
Good to know
Works best on leather — mention the material.
Quote this

Clean, dye & polish

Fixes
Scuffs, faded colour, salt marks and dried-out leather.
Good to know
A photo in daylight shows the true colour best.
Quote this

Boots & work boots

Fixes
Worn heels and soles, split welts and tired leather on favourite boots.
Good to know
Photograph the sole and the side of the boot.
Quote this

Bags, belts & leather

Fixes
Torn straps, broken buckles, extra belt holes and loose stitching.
Good to know
Photograph the broken part up close.
