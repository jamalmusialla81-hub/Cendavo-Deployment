# Cendavo business launch notes

Analysis by the supervisor, 2026-10-11. No customer, conversion or cost data exists yet; every number below is an assumption to validate, not a forecast.

## Are the historical prices sustainable?

Historical ideas: website A$199–399 upfront, maintenance A$29/month, higher-value recurring from A$79/month.

| Route to A$10,000/month | What it takes at historical prices |
| --- | --- |
| Maintenance only at A$29 | ≈ 345 paying clients |
| New sites only at A$299 | ≈ 34 sites delivered **every month** |

A real small-business site still needs discovery, copy, images, revisions, launch, DNS/email help and support. Even with Forge doing much of the build, 8–15 hours per site is realistic, so A$299 is roughly A$20–37/hour before payment fees and tax, and the volume needed is beyond one person plus outreach. **Not sustainable** except as a short-term portfolio-building offer (e.g. 3 founding clients at a disclosed discount in exchange for a case study).

Illustrative month at ≈ A$10k: 3 Starter (A$2,970) + 1 Business (A$2,200) + 1 Redesign (A$1,500) + 30 Care plans × A$99 (A$2,970) ≈ **A$9,640**. The care-plan base is what makes income stable; it compounds only if churn stays low.

## Costs to account for

- Hosting: Cloudflare Pages / D1 free tier is enough for small sites (client domains ≈ A$20–40/year, passed through).
- Payment processing: Stripe domestic cards ≈ 1.7% + A$0.30 per payment.
- Software: Claude subscription (already held), design assets, email.
- **GST:** registration is required once turnover reaches A$75,000/year; A$10k/month is A$120k/year, so prices must state "ex GST" or "inc GST" correctly from the start. Get accounting advice on ABN, GST and income tax.
- Insurance (professional indemnity) and simple written terms (scope, revisions, ownership, care-plan cancellation).
- Customer acquisition: time spent on Lead Hunter research and manual outreach (Spam Act 2003 rules apply to commercial email/SMS).

## Sales path the website must support

Discover Cendavo → understand services → see examples (the labelled demo now; real client work as it exists) → understand starting prices → request a consultation (task A enquiry form) → qualified lead in D1 with status tracking.
