## Educational social post 1
Three things to check on your business website today:
1. Can a phone visitor see what service you offer without scrolling forever?
2. Is the contact button visible and easy to use?
3. Do your photos show work you've actually done?

If any answer is no, you may not need a whole new website — start by fixing the basics. We explain our approach at https://cendavo.au/services/
