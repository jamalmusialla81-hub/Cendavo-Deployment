Built new. Rebuilt properly. Looked after.

Three ways we work with local service businesses. Most people start with a new site or a redesign, then keep us on to look after it.

  • New websiteNo site yet, or one that was never finished
  • RedesignA site that looks dated or doesn't bring in work

New website

For businesses with no website, a one-page placeholder, or a site someone started and never finished. We plan the pages around how you win work, write the words with you and build it to bring in enquiries.

Who it's for
Sole traders and small teams who get most of their work by word of mouth and want somewhere good to send people.
What's included
  • A plan of the pages, agreed before design starts
  • Design for phone first, then tablet and desktop
  • Words written with you, in plain language
  • Your photos, cropped and optimised
  • An enquiry form that sends to your inbox
  • Basic search setup: page titles, descriptions and a sitemap
  • Domain connection and launch
How long it takes
Most small sites take a few weeks from the first call to launch. The biggest factor is usually how quickly words and photos come together.
What we need from you
  • An hour or so to talk about your business and customers
  • Photos of your work, if you have them
  • Your logo, if you have one
  • Feedback on the design and the words when we send them

Redesign

Keep your name, your domain and the trust you've built. Replace the dated look, the slow pages and the contact form nobody fills in.

Who it's for
Businesses whose site is more than a few years old, hard to use on a phone, or embarrassing to hand out.
What's included
  • A review of your current site: what works, what to cut
  • Everything in a new website, rebuilt on a fast setup
  • Your existing words and photos, kept or improved
  • Old page addresses redirected, so links and search listings keep working
  • A move off your old hosting, if you want one
How long it takes
Usually a little quicker than a new site, because some of the content already exists. We'll give you a realistic timeline in your quote.
What we need from you
  • Access to your current site, domain and hosting, or the name of whoever manages them
  • What customers ask you most often
  • Which parts of the old site you like, if any

Care plan

A website isn't finished at launch. Software needs updating, opening hours change, and someone needs to notice if the enquiry form stops working. On a care plan, that someone is us.

Every month

  • UpdatesSoftware and security updates applied and checked.
  • BackupsCopies of your site kept, so it can be restored if something breaks.
  • Form checkWe check that enquiries from your site still reach your inbox.
  • Small changesNew hours, prices, photos or a staff change, done for you.
Who it's for
Owners who would rather spend their evenings on anything other than their website. It suits sites we built, and sites we've taken over after a review.
How long it takes
It starts the day your site goes live, or after a short check if we didn't build it. We agree what counts as a small change when we quote, so there are no surprises.
What we need from you
An email when something changes in your business. That's it.

Questions owners ask us

Something else on your mind? Ask it in your enquiry.

Do I need my words and photos ready before we start?

No. Most people don't. We'll ask you questions on the call and draft the words from your answers. If you don't have good photos yet, we'll tell you which ones would help most, and you can take many of them on a phone.

Can I keep my current domain name?

Yes. Your domain stays yours. We connect it to the new site and, on a redesign, redirect old page addresses so existing links keep working.

Can I make changes to the site myself?

If you want to, we'll talk about it on the call and set things up so the parts you change often are easy to edit. Many owners prefer to send us an email instead, which is what the care plan is for.

My site is with another company. Can you still help?

Usually, yes. We'll need access to the site, domain and hosting, or the details of whoever manages them. We'll look at what you have before suggesting a redesign or a care plan.

Do I have to take a care plan?

No. It's optional. If you'd rather look after the site yourself, we'll hand everything over at launch and show you what needs regular attention.

Do you only work with Sydney businesses?

We're based in Sydney and focus on local businesses here, which is why the concept projects are Sydney businesses. If you're somewhere else, get in touch anyway and we'll tell you honestly whether we're a good fit.

Not sure which one you need?

That's what the free consultation is for. Tell us what you have now and what you'd like it to do, and we'll recommend the simplest option.
