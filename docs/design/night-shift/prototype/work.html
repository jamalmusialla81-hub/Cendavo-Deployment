Harbourside Mobile Detailing

Trade
Mobile car detailing
Area
Eastern suburbs and inner west
Pages
Home, packages, booking, areas, FAQ

The brief

Customers kept texting the same three questions: how long does it take, do you come to me, and when are you free? The site had to answer all three before anyone picked up the phone.

What the site does for the business

  • Leads with the finish. A full-width photo of a car mid-wash does more selling than a paragraph of adjectives.
  • Explains packages by time. Express wash, full detail and paint correction, each with how long it takes, so customers can plan their day.
  • Books in two steps. Choose a package and a time, add the address. No account, no back-and-forth texts.
  • Lists the suburbs served up front, so nobody books a job across the bridge by mistake.

Greenline Landscapes

Trade
Landscape design and construction
Area
North Shore
Pages
Home, projects, services, site visit

The brief

Greenline's jobs are big and their photos are beautiful, but every quote started with a wasted drive. They wanted the website to show the finished work, then collect enough detail that the first site visit is a real one.

What the site does for the business

  • Lets finished projects sell. Each project has its suburb, what was built and a before and after.
  • Asks the right questions first. The site-visit form asks about the space, access and timing, and lets people attach photos from their phone.
  • Loads quickly on a weak signal, because people often look it up standing in their own backyard.

Fade & Co

Trade
Barbershop, two chairs
Area
Inner west
Pages
Home, services, book, find us

The brief

Most people who look up a barber want three things: are you open, how long is the wait, and can I book? Fade & Co's old page made them scroll past a gallery to find out.

  • Today's hours on the first screen, updated automatically from the opening times.
  • Choose your barber or take the first free chair. Regulars can stay with the person who knows their cut.
  • Directions one tap away, with a note about where to park.

Demonstration project — sample data

A request queue that runs in your browser

Not a client website. We built this small app to show the kind of tool we can make: add a sample customer request, move it from new to follow-up to done, and prepare a reply draft you would check and send yourself.

Everything uses made-up names and stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent to Cendavo or anyone else.
