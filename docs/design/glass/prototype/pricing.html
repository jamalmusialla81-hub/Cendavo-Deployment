What affects the quote

Four things make the biggest difference. At the consultation we work out where your business sits on each, and the quote reflects exactly that.

  • Number of pages

    A single page can be enough for a sole trader. A business with several services, or several suburbs, usually needs a page for each so customers land on exactly what they searched for.

    One pageA page per service and suburb

  • Booking and integrations

    An enquiry form is included. Online booking, a calendar connection, or links to tools you already use add setup and testing time.

    Enquiry formBooking linked to your tools

  • Copywriting

    If you already have words you’re happy with, we edit and lay them out. If not, we write every page from a conversation with you.

    You supply the wordsWe write every page with you

  • Photography

    Good photos of real work sell better than anything we can write. Using photos you have is simplest; if new ones are needed, we’ll plan what to shoot and how.

    Photos you already haveNew photos needed first

Straight answers

About money, mostly.

Why don’t you list prices?

Because a one-page site for an electrician and a booking site for a barbershop are very different jobs. A price list would either overcharge one of them or surprise the other. A short consultation lets us quote what you actually need.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes, with no obligation. If we don’t think we’re the right fit, we’ll say so.

Can the price change after I accept the quote?

Not for the work in the quote. If you ask for something new partway through, we’ll tell you what it changes and agree it with you before we do it.

Are there any ongoing costs?

Your domain name renews every year with whoever it’s registered with, and some hosting or booking tools have their own fees. Your quote lists anything like this so nothing comes as a surprise.

Do I have to take the care plan?

No. It’s quoted separately and entirely optional.
