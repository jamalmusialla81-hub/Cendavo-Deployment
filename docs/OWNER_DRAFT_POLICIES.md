These are placeholder business policies written at the owner's request so the Pricing and About pages can explain the process. They are commitments to customers once published: the owner must confirm or edit each line before the site goes live. Nothing here is a price, a statistic, a testimonial or a claim about past clients.

## Process and pricing
- Free consultation: a 20–30 minute call or text about the business, what customers need to find, and what the site should do. No obligation.
- Fixed quote: sent in writing after the consultation, listing pages, features and what's needed from the client. The price doesn't change unless the client asks to add something; any addition is quoted first.
- Payment: 50% to start, 50% before launch.
- Revisions: two rounds of revisions on the design, and one round on the built site, are included. Further changes are quoted.
- What's included by default: mobile-first design, the agreed pages, an enquiry form that sends to the client, basic on-page SEO (titles, descriptions, sitemap), image optimisation, setup on fast static hosting, and a handover walkthrough.
- What the client provides: logo (if they have one), photos of their work (or we help choose/edit), service details and prices they want shown.
- Timeline: most small sites take 2–4 weeks from approved quote, depending on content being ready.
- Ownership: the client owns their domain and content; the site is set up in their name.

## Recurring costs
- Domain renewal: paid by the client to their registrar (we help set it up).
- Hosting: static hosting on Cloudflare's free tier for most small sites; if a site needs paid features, that's quoted upfront.
- Care plan: optional, monthly, cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. Covers updates, backups, form checks and small content changes (up to an agreed amount each month). Price quoted with the project.

## Communication
- We reply to enquiries within one business day.
