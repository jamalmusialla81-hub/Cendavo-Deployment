How it works

Five steps, one point of contact. You see and approve everything before it goes live.

  1. Discover

    You tell us about your business and customers in a free consultation. We ask the questions that shape the site.

  2. Design

    We design your key pages around your work. You review them and tell us what to change.

  3. Build

    We build the site so it reads properly on phones. You send photos and check the words.

  4. Launch

    We put the site live on your domain. You start sending customers to it.

  5. Look after

    If you choose a care plan, we keep the site up to date. You just ask when something needs changing.

Questions

Do I need to have everything ready before we talk?

No. A few sentences about your business is enough to start. We work out together what your website needs.

Can you redesign the website I already have?

Yes. We look at what your current site does well, keep what is worth keeping and rebuild the rest.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly photos and words come together. We include a timeline with your quote.

What happens after the site goes live?

You can look after it yourself or ask us about a care plan. We explain the options during your consultation.
