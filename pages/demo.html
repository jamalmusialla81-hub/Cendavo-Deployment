Demonstration project, sample data

A request queue that runs in your browser.

Not a client website. We built this small app to show the kind of tool we can make: capture a sample request, track its status and prepare a follow-up draft.

Demo only. Use fictional names and details. Data is stored in this browser; no enquiry or message is sent to Cendavo or anyone else.
