When to improve your existing website

Start with the problems, not the appearance. Is your phone number or enquiry form difficult to find? Do your services sound like they were written years ago? Are pages slow or hard to read on mobile? These are useful reasons to review a site.

A redesign doesn't always require starting again. We first discuss what is working, what could be kept and which pages genuinely need rebuilding. For a Sydney service business, the priority is usually making your real work, service area and next step easier to understand.

If the website already attracts visitors, preserving valuable page addresses and checking redirects during a rebuild are important. We make recommendations after reviewing your existing site rather than claiming every redesign will increase enquiries.
