What ongoing website care looks like

A website can become outdated even when nothing is broken. Perhaps you change opening hours, add a service, replace photos or need help after a platform update. Maintenance is about keeping the information useful and the site working as intended.

Cendavo offers website care plans for local Sydney service businesses. The scope depends on the site: agreed small content changes, updates and backups can be included, with responsibilities and limits written into your quote.

We can care for websites we build, or discuss taking over a site built elsewhere after reviewing its platform and access. We do not promise unlimited changes or guaranteed security outcomes; you should know what is and isn't included before agreeing.
