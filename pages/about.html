A small Sydney studio for local service businesses.

Cendavo designs, builds and looks after websites for tradespeople, cleaners, detailers, barbers and other local operators. Why? Because good businesses lose work to weak websites: pages that crawl on a phone, photos that hide the work, and no clear way to get in touch.

How we think

Four ideas shape every site we design. They are simple on purpose: each one is about helping a customer decide to get in touch with you.

  • Built for the phone first

    Many of your customers will look you up on a phone, often in a hurry. We design for that small screen first, then scale up for tablets and desktops.

  • Your real work up front

    Photos of jobs you have actually done say more than any stock image. We put your work, your services and the areas you cover where people see them straight away.

  • One clear next step on every page

    Every page ends with one obvious way to get in touch, so nobody has to hunt for a phone number or a form.

  • Fast and accessible by default

    Pages that load quickly, read clearly and work with a keyboard or a screen reader. We build that in from the first page, not as an extra at the end.

How we build

The technical choices behind every site, in plain terms. They keep pages quick for your customers and running costs low for you.

  • Static sites on Cloudflare.Modern static pages served from Cloudflare’s global network: quick to load, with few moving parts to break or attack, and low running costs.
  • Accessible markup.Real headings, labelled forms, visible focus and readable contrast, so the site works with a keyboard and a screen reader.
  • Self-hosted fonts.Fonts are served from your own site rather than a third-party font service, so pages load faster and visitors aren’t passed to anyone else.
  • No tracking scripts by default.This site loads none, and we don’t add analytics to yours unless you ask for them.
